# Master Supply Agreement — Project Delta

**Effective Date:** January 12, 2024
**Vendor:** Supplier ABC
**Client:** Northwind Industries
**Project:** Project Delta — fabrication of 24 turbine blade assemblies.

## Scope

Supplier ABC shall manufacture and deliver 24 turbine blade assemblies meeting drawing revision R3 for Project Delta. The first lot of 8 units is due 2024-04-15; the remaining 16 units are due 2024-06-30.

## Pricing

Total contract value: USD 1,800,000. Invoiced in three installments — 30% on signing, 30% on first lot delivery, 40% on final acceptance.

## Signatures

This agreement is signed this 12th day of January 2024 by John Carter (Procurement Director, Northwind) and Maria Lopez (VP Operations, Supplier ABC). Signed copies retained by both parties.
