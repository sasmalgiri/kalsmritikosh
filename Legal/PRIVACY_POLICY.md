## Summary (plain language)
Kalsmritikosh is **private by design**. Your documents, the structured knowledge
it builds from them, and your questions **stay on your device**. We do not run a
server that receives your content, and the app contains **no analytics or
telemetry**. Because of that, for the core product **we do not collect, transmit,
store, or have any access to your personal data.**

## 1. What data the app processes, and where
- **Your documents and archives** that you choose to add. These are read and
  indexed **on your device only**. They are never uploaded to us or any third
  party by the app.
- **The derived knowledge** (entities, events, timelines, summaries, datasets)
  and **your questions and answers** — all created and stored **locally** in the
  app's sandboxed data folder.
- All AI processing (reasoning, search, extraction, OCR, transcription) runs
  **on-device** using bundled/on-device models.

## 2. What we collect
For the core, offline product: **nothing.** We operate no backend that receives
your content, and the app ships with no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and
no telemetry.

If the documents you ingest contain other people's personal data, **you** are the
controller of that data. You are responsible for having the lawful right to

All data lives in the app's local sandbox. **You control retention.** You can
delete everything by removing the app's data folder or deleting the app. We hold
