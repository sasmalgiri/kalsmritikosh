## 4. THIRTY natural questions, your own words
A mix of who / what / when / how many / list / total / why / mixed
("was X before Y"). Sort every result:
- **Answered** — cited and correct.
- **Marked** — partial, inferred, two-lane, or declined honestly.
- **Wrong** — anything incorrect or misleading.

**Every *Wrong* is a STOP: permanent case → fix → reseal → re-witness
before submission.** Every Tier-1 question landing in *Marked* → filed
with priority. (This page is the triage sheet — tally on it.)
