## Twin-disagreement rate (advisory quality signal, per component)

The verification twins are checkers, never writers: their verdicts land
as advisory review rows and log counters, excluded from every sealed
envelope. The rates are measured on the live ledger, per reviewer:

| Component | Where measured |
|---|---|
| Entity plausibility twin | fact_reviews rows, reviewer `twin.entity` (flag ÷ scanned) |
| Event record twin | fact_reviews rows, reviewer `twin.event` (flag ÷ examined) |
| Story placement twin | fact_reviews rows, reviewer `twin.placement` (flag ÷ artifacts) |
| Compose twin | log counters `twin.compose` (disagreed ÷ run) |

A rising rate is a RULE-DEFECT signal for that component — findings feed
review, never auto-tuning.

## Break-budgets (I-9) — adversarial groups, every break a permanent case

| Promise group | Budget | Suite |
|---|---|---|
| Defeat the abstention | 5 cases | KalsmritikoshTests/BreakBudgetTests (abstention group) |
| Smuggle a digit | 5 cases | KalsmritikoshTests/BreakBudgetTests (digit group) |
| Mis-anchor a milestone | 5 cases | KalsmritikoshTests/BreakBudgetTests (anchor group) |

A group is CLOSED only when its budget is spent with no open break; any
future break joins the suite permanently before the fix ships.
