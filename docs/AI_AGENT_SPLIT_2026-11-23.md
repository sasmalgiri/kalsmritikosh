# AI does the thinking, the app does the exact work (owner, 2026-11-23)

**The owner's decision.** AI is mandatory: Claude, on the person's own key or the owner's relay. The AI does all the understanding and judgement. The app does what software does well: open every format, OCR, exact search, fingerprints and custody, exact arithmetic, dates and counts, store every finding with its source, verify quotes and numbers mechanically, privacy, consent and audit, scale and speed, memory, exports and teams. Anything that is no longer needed is switched off: reversibly, by flag, and never by deleting data.

**Evidence for the decision.** The 10 real solved cases (docs/evidence/agent-eval-2026-11-23/REPORT.md) showed where the app's rule-based "understanding" goes wrong or adds noise:
- entity labels;
- co-occurrence treated as a relationship;
- amounts that lose their scale;
- a person-versus-company veto that overrides the AI;
- 0 contradictions found by the rules;
- a 1-item rules timeline;
- false "Needs review" flags.

The full classification of every module, engine and flag (Keep, AI, Combine, Off) came from a code sweep on 2026-11-23. It is summarised in the tracker rows below; each row lists the call sites to change.
