What Kalsmritikosh is

A private, on-device app that turns a folder of your documents and emails into a searchable evidence ledger and reconstructed timeline, and answers questions with citations you can open. Everything runs on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, ever.

Requirements

  • macOS 15.6 or later (AI-written answers require macOS 26 or later).
  • Apple Intelligence enabled — the app uses Apple's on-device model to write answers. You can still ingest, browse, search, and view evidence without it; only the composed-answer step needs it.

Reporting a problem

Email the address above with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and (optionally) an exported diagnostics file (Settings → Diagnostics). Diagnostics may contain file paths/content, so review before sending — it is never sent automatically.

Accuracy

Kalsmritikosh assists analysis; it is not legal/financial/professional advice and makes no admissibility claim. Verify material facts independently. See the EULA.
