Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Publisher: Shrabani Sasmal · Contact: sasmalgiri@gmail.com
The short version. Investigations Made Easy is a private, on-device application. In Private mode — the default — your documents, the knowledge built from them, your questions, and the answers never leave your Mac. We run no servers that keep your content, in any mode. We do not have accounts, tracking, ads, analytics, or telemetry. If you turn on Online mode and use AI — with an AI plan (AI Included or AI Pro) or your own Anthropic key — what you approve goes from your Mac to a third-party AI: for a case you allow, that case's documents and your questions about it go to Anthropic (Claude); a recording you choose to transcribe goes to Google (Gemini). On an AI plan it passes through our relay, which passes it on and keeps only counts; with your own key it goes directly. The AI provider keeps it under its own terms (below). You give that permission once per case, and nothing is sent without it.

What the app processes, and where

  • Your files. You select folders/files. The app reads them in place on your Mac to build a local index (a single on-device database). Files are not copied to us or anyone else.
  • Derived data (text excerpts, entities, dates, relationships, embeddings, summaries, answers) is stored in the app's encrypted local database inside your user Library container. The search-index cache of embeddings beside it is encrypted too, with a key derived from the database key. A few working files stay beside it without the database's encryption (protected by your Mac's FileVault only if you have it turned on): import receipts, captured items waiting to be read, diagnostics, and — if you turn them on — managed copies of originals (readable only by your user account) and Spotlight entries. Removing the app's data removes those beside the database; backups, snapshots, sync folders and exports you made, the Keychain items and the Spotlight entries are separate, and you remove them yourself.
  • AI models run on-device. Reasoning and embedding models execute locally; in Private mode your content is never sent to a cloud model.
  • Encrypted at rest. The local database is encrypted on your Mac (AES-256, through Apple's CommonCrypto). Its key is kept in your Mac's Keychain and is shown to you once as a recovery key, which you need to open the database on another Mac. We never receive the key.

Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. In Private mode no document text, questions, answers, filenames, or usage data are transmitted for any purpose, so they never leave your Mac, and there is no analytics or crash-telemetry SDK. The only network activity is ordinary system behaviour when you open a cited source file or a link in another app. The app itself has no network permission at all; only a separate, sandboxed helper inside it can connect, and only when you turn on Online mode. Diagnostics (MetricKit reports from macOS) stay on your Mac and are never sent automatically; you can look at them and choose to email them to us.

Online mode (optional)

Online mode is off unless you turn it on (Settings ▸ Modules ▸ Connection). You then choose how AI is reached: an AI plan (AI Included or AI Pro, below), or your own Anthropic API key (Claude — the only provider available with your own key), stored in your Mac's Keychain.

Third-party AI reading a case — your permission, once per case. The first time you use AI inside a case, the app shows a permission sheet listing exactly which of that case's documents would be sent to Anthropic (Claude), which would not and why, their size and the estimated cost (in credits on an AI plan, in dollars on your own key). Only if you choose Allow for this case are those documents, and the questions you then ask in that case, sent to Anthropic so that Claude can read them and answer — on an AI plan through our relay, with your own key directly. You are asked once for each case, and asked again whenever its documents change (a document added, removed or replaced). If a case is too large to read whole, the same permission covers sending, for each question, the passages of those documents most related to it. Not now sends nothing; the app then answers from your documents on your Mac. Inside a case you allowed, your questions are sent without a further prompt (each is still masked if you turned masking on, and recorded); anything outside the documents you allowed is shown to you first. A recording you choose to transcribe is sent to Google (Gemini) through our relay on an AI plan, after you confirm that send.

What is never sent: material marked privileged is never sent, nor the identities of protected sources, nor the documents of any other case, nor anything in Private mode. Online mode's other parts each have their own switch. Understanding a question's wording sends only your question and the names of the fields your documents hold — never document text. Letting your cloud AI read the documents about a person across your whole archive (off until you turn it on) sends up to eight of the documents that name that person, up to 7,000 characters of each, and is asked for each time. Every send is recorded on your Mac (Settings ▸ Online ▸ What was sent).

Document text goes only to Claude on Anthropic's commercial API — with your own key after you confirm your account is on those terms, or through an AI plan; with your own key no other AI provider is used in this release. The provider keeps what it receives under its own terms: Anthropic's commercial API deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, with stated exceptions, and does not use them for training; on an AI plan, recordings go to Google's paid Gemini API under our account. The app shows these terms when it asks for your permission.

Before any AI send, the app first shows you exactly what would be sent — inside a case, the case's documents, once, as above (after that, your questions in that case are sent without a further prompt); elsewhere, the passages and your question each time — with the destination and its size, and nothing is sent until you agree. You can have names, email addresses, phone numbers and long identifiers replaced by placeholders first (masking), in the documents and in your questions alike. With your own key, the text goes directly from your Mac to Anthropic, under your own account and Anthropic's terms and privacy policy, and never passes through any server of ours. Every send is recorded on your Mac. Only a separate, sandboxed helper inside the app can connect, over HTTPS, and only to the destinations listed on this page. One click returns you to Private mode.

AI Included and AI Pro

With an AI Included or AI Pro plan, approved requests go from your Mac to our relay, which checks with Apple that your AI plan is active and passes the request on to Anthropic's commercial API (or, for transcribing a recording, to Google's paid Gemini API) under our account. The relay identifies you only by the App Store transaction number of the subscription — no name, email or Apple ID. It keeps only counts and credits: the credits you used this month and each day, the credit packs you bought and what is left of them, and how much the service spent. It never stores or logs a question, a passage, a recording or an answer. Anthropic and Google keep what they receive under their commercial terms (Anthropic deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, with stated exceptions; neither uses them for training). Every AI plan send needs your permission exactly as with your own key — shown first with its estimated cost, or, inside a case you allowed, covered by that permission — and is recorded on your Mac.

Where generative AI services may not be offered — mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, Russia, Belarus, Venezuela, Afghanistan, Myanmar, Yemen, Iran, North Korea, Syria and Cuba in this release — the app switches Online AI off for App Store accounts of that country: the AI plans and credit packs are not sold there (only Workbench is), and your own key cannot be used either. The country is the one Apple sells in for your Apple account; the app itself does not read your location or IP address. The AI Included relay, which runs on Cloudflare, also refuses requests that arrive from those countries: Cloudflare sees each request's IP address and tells the relay only the country it comes from, which the relay uses for that check and does not keep.

Online tools (optional)

In Online mode, Sources ▸ Online tools can look things up in public records. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank (the currency and date are sent). Sanctions checks download the complete public OFAC and UN lists and search them on your Mac — the name you check is not sent. Company-registry (GLEIF) and court-record (CourtListener) searches send the name you search to that service. A web capture fetches the https page you enter, from your network. Recording transcription sends the recording through your AI plan (AI Included or AI Pro) to Google Gemini. Each of these is shown to you before it is sent (the list downloads carry nothing of yours) and recorded on your Mac.

Subscription, trial and purchase

The subscription (Workbench, AI Included or AI Pro), Workbench's free introductory trial, credit packs, renewals, refunds and restores are handled by Apple through the App Store. Investigations Made Easy itself opens no connection for them and never sees your payment details; the App Store only tells the app which verified plan or purchase this Apple account holds and until when. Subscribing turns nothing else on: it does not enable Online mode or send anything anywhere. When you choose an AI plan, the app asks the relay only whether the AI plans are open to new subscribers this month; checking your credits or adding a credit pack sends only the transaction numbers. With your own key, the app keeps a tally of the cost Anthropic reports on your Mac only, for the monthly limit you may set. Apple's role: Apple sells and bills the plans and credit packs, handles cancellation and refunds, and — for the AI plans — answers our relay's check of which plan a transaction number holds. Apple's own privacy policy covers what Apple keeps; Apple does not receive your documents or questions from this app.

Sync through a folder (optional)

If you turn on sync, the app writes your notes, tags, groups, highlights, PDF marks and the files you added inside the app into a folder you choose, encrypted with your passphrase. That folder's own service (for example iCloud Drive or Dropbox) moves the files between your devices; the app itself makes no connection, and the passphrase is never stored.

Safari clipper

The Safari clipper downloads nothing: it hands the page your browser already shows, or your selection, to the app on your Mac, and only when you clip.

Share menu, Services and the iPhone/iPad viewer

Anything you send to Investigations Made Easy from the Share or Services menu is saved into the app's own folder on your Mac with a record of when and from where; nothing is downloaded. A snapshot you export for the iPhone/iPad viewer is a folder you carry yourself (AirDrop, Files, a disk); its database is encrypted with a passphrase you set, and the viewer only reads it.

Data you export

Any export you trigger (answers, reports, diagnostics) is written to a location you choose or an app-private folder. Exports may contain your source content and file paths — they are created only on your explicit action and are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Files & folders: to read the archive you choose (macOS security-scoped bookmarks). You can revoke access anytime in macOS Settings.

Children

The app is for adults: it is rated 18+, and its Online AI and Online tools may only be used by people 18 or older (the AI providers' terms require it). It is not directed to children and we do not knowingly receive anything from them.

Who is responsible, and your rights

The publisher, Shrabani Sasmal (contact below), is responsible for the AI relay and the counts it keeps. Those counts — the AI subscription's App Store transaction number, the credits used each month and each day, credit packs bought and their remaining credits, and the service's monthly spend — are kept to provide the service you subscribed to (performance of a contract): monthly counts for 13 months, daily counts for 40 days, and a credit pack for as long as it has credits left (packs never expire) and then 25 months after purchase; then they are deleted. The relay runs on Cloudflare; the App Store is Apple's; AI Included requests are processed by Anthropic and Google as described above. Some of these providers are in the United States, so data may be processed outside your country. You may ask what the relay holds about your subscription number, and to have it corrected or deleted (deleting it ends the AI plan's service for that subscription and removes any remaining pack credits), by writing to the contact below with your App Store order number. Everything else stays on your Mac, where you control and can delete it (Settings ▸ Your data); the record of what was sent is kept in the app's database on your Mac, and each case's AI permission in the app's settings on your Mac (a fingerprint of the case's document versions and a count — never their content). What Anthropic or Google already received is kept and deleted under their terms (Anthropic's commercial API deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, with stated exceptions); we cannot recall it from them.
