Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026 · Publisher: Shrabani Sasmal · Contact: sasmalgiri@gmail.com
The short version. Investigations Made Easy is a private, on-device application. In Private mode — the default — your documents, the knowledge built from them, your questions, and the answers never leave your Mac. We run no servers that keep your content, in any mode. We do not have accounts, tracking, ads, analytics, or telemetry. If you turn on Online mode, what you approve goes from your Mac to the AI provider you chose — or, with AI Included, through our relay, which passes it on and keeps only counts — and that provider keeps it under its own terms (below).

What the app processes, and where

  • Your files. You select folders/files. The app reads them in place on your Mac to build a local index (a single on-device database). Files are not copied to us or anyone else.
  • Derived data (text excerpts, entities, dates, relationships, embeddings, summaries, answers) is stored in the app's encrypted local database inside your user Library container. The search-index cache of embeddings beside it is encrypted too, with a key derived from the database key. A few working files stay beside it without the database's encryption (protected by your Mac's FileVault only if you have it turned on): import receipts, captured items waiting to be read, diagnostics, and — if you turn them on — managed copies of originals (readable only by your user account) and Spotlight entries. Removing the app's data removes those beside the database; backups, snapshots, sync folders and exports you made, the Keychain items and the Spotlight entries are separate, and you remove them yourself.
  • AI models run on-device. Reasoning and embedding models execute locally; in Private mode your content is never sent to a cloud model.
  • Encrypted at rest. The local database is encrypted on your Mac (AES-256, through Apple's CommonCrypto). Its key is kept in your Mac's Keychain and is shown to you once as a recovery key, which you need to open the database on another Mac. We never receive the key.

Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. In Private mode no document text, questions, answers, filenames, or usage data are transmitted for any purpose, so they never leave your Mac, and there is no analytics or crash-telemetry SDK. The only network activity is ordinary system behaviour when you open a cited source file or a link in another app.

Online mode (optional)

Online mode is off unless you turn it on (Settings ▸ Modules ▸ Connection). You then choose how AI is reached: AI Included (part of the AI Included plans, below), or your own API key for a cloud AI provider you choose — Anthropic, OpenAI or Google — stored in your Mac's Keychain.

Online mode has separate parts, each with its own switch. Understanding a question's wording sends only your question and the names of the fields your documents hold — never document text. Letting your cloud AI read the documents about a person (off until you turn it on) sends up to eight of the documents that name that person, up to 7,000 characters of each; material marked privileged is never sent. Every send is shown to you first and recorded.

Document text goes only to a provider whose terms permit case material — Claude on Anthropic's commercial API, or a paid Gemini project — after you confirm your account is on those terms; no document text goes to OpenAI in this release. The provider keeps what it receives under its own terms: Anthropic's commercial API deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, with stated exceptions; Google's unpaid Gemini tier may use content and have it reviewed by people, which is why case documents are not sent to an unpaid key. The app shows these terms with every send of document text, and asks every time.

When an answer would be worded by that provider, the app first shows you exactly what would be sent — passages from your documents and your question, the destination and its size — and nothing is sent until you agree. You can have names, email addresses, phone numbers and long identifiers replaced by placeholders first. With your own key, the text goes directly from your Mac to that provider, under your own account and that provider's terms and privacy policy, and never passes through any server of ours. Every send is recorded on your Mac. Only a separate, sandboxed helper inside the app can connect, over HTTPS, and only to the destinations listed on this page. One click returns you to Private mode.

AI Included

With an AI Included plan, approved requests go from your Mac to our relay, which checks with Apple that your AI Included subscription is active and passes the request on to Anthropic's commercial API (or, for transcribing a recording, to Google's paid Gemini API) under our account. The relay identifies you only by the App Store transaction number of the subscription — no name, email or Apple ID. It keeps only counts: how many questions you used this month, the top-ups you bought, and how much the service spent. It never stores or logs a question, a passage, a recording or an answer. Anthropic and Google keep what they receive under their commercial terms (Anthropic deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days by default, with stated exceptions; neither uses them for training). Every AI Included send is shown to you first and recorded on your Mac, exactly as with your own key.

Where generative AI services may not be offered — mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao, Russia, Belarus, Venezuela, Afghanistan, Myanmar, Yemen, Iran, North Korea, Syria and Cuba in this release — the app switches Online AI off for App Store accounts of that country. The country is the one Apple sells in for your Apple account; the app does not read your location or IP address.

Online tools (optional)

In Online mode, Sources ▸ Online tools can look things up in public records. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank (the currency and date are sent). Sanctions checks download the complete public OFAC and UN lists and search them on your Mac — the name you check is not sent. Company-registry (GLEIF) and court-record (CourtListener) searches send the name you search to that service. A web capture fetches the https page you enter, from your network. Recording transcription sends the recording to AI Included or to your own Gemini key. Each of these is shown to you before it is sent (the list downloads carry nothing of yours) and recorded on your Mac.

Subscription, trial and purchase

The subscription, its free introductory trial, renewals, refunds, restores and any earlier one-time purchase are handled by Apple through the App Store. Investigations Made Easy itself opens no connection for them and never sees your payment details; the App Store only tells the app which verified plan or purchase this Apple account holds and until when. Subscribing turns nothing else on: it does not enable Online mode or send anything anywhere. When you choose an AI Included plan, the app asks the relay only whether AI Included is open to new subscribers this month; checking your remaining questions or adding a top-up sends only the subscription's transaction number.

Sync through a folder (optional)

If you turn on sync, the app writes your notes, tags, groups, highlights, PDF marks and the files you added inside the app into a folder you choose, encrypted with your passphrase. That folder's own service (for example iCloud Drive or Dropbox) moves the files between your devices; the app itself makes no connection, and the passphrase is never stored.

Safari clipper

The Safari clipper downloads nothing: it hands the page your browser already shows, or your selection, to the app on your Mac, and only when you clip.

Share menu, Services and the iPhone/iPad viewer

Anything you send to Investigations Made Easy from the Share or Services menu is saved into the app's own folder on your Mac with a record of when and from where; nothing is downloaded. A snapshot you export for the iPhone/iPad viewer is a folder you carry yourself (AirDrop, Files, a disk); its database is encrypted with a passphrase you set, and the viewer only reads it.

Data you export

Any export you trigger (answers, reports, diagnostics) is written to a location you choose or an app-private folder. Exports may contain your source content and file paths — they are created only on your explicit action and are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Files & folders: to read the archive you choose (macOS security-scoped bookmarks). You can revoke access anytime in macOS Settings.

Children

The app is for adults: it is rated 18+, and its Online AI and Online tools may only be used by people 18 or older (the AI providers' terms require it).

Who is responsible, and your rights

The publisher, Shrabani Sasmal (contact below), is responsible for the AI Included relay and the counts it keeps. Those counts — the AI Included subscription's App Store transaction number, the questions used each month, top-ups bought and the service's monthly spend — are kept to provide the service you subscribed to (performance of a contract) for as long as the subscription is active and then for 13 months, and are then deleted. The relay runs on Cloudflare; the App Store is Apple's; AI Included requests are processed by Anthropic and Google as described above. Some of these providers are in the United States, so data may be processed outside your country. You may ask what the relay holds about your subscription number, and to have it corrected or deleted (deleting it ends AI Included for that subscription), by writing to the contact below with your App Store order number. Everything else stays on your Mac, where you control and can delete it (Settings ▸ Your data).
