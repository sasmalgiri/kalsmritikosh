Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Publisher: Eco Sanskriti Innovation · Contact: sasmalgiri@gmail.com
The short version. Kalsmritikosh is a private, on-device application. Your documents, the knowledge built from them, your questions, and the answers never leave your Mac. We do not operate servers that receive your content. We do not have accounts, tracking, ads, analytics, or telemetry. The App Store privacy label (as declared) is Data Not Collected.

What the app processes, and where

  • Your files. You select folders/files. The app reads them in place on your Mac to build a local index (a single on-device database). Files are not copied to us or anyone else.
  • Derived data (text excerpts, entities, dates, relationships, embeddings, summaries, answers) is stored only in the app's local database inside your user Library container. Deleting the app or using the in-app "Delete data" removes it. If you enabled the optional managed-evidence copies or made exports, delete those from your disk as well.
  • AI models run on-device. Reasoning and embedding models execute locally; your content is never sent to a cloud model. The v1 release contains no cloud reasoning path.

Network use

The app is designed to work fully offline. No document text, questions, answers, filenames, or usage data are transmitted for any purpose, and there is no analytics or crash-telemetry SDK. The only network activity is ordinary system behaviour when you open a cited source file or a link in another app.

Data you export

Any export you trigger (answers, reports, diagnostics) is written to a location you choose or an app-private folder. Exports may contain your source content and file paths — they are created only on your explicit action and are never uploaded.

Permissions

  • Files & folders: to read the archive you choose (macOS security-scoped bookmarks). You can revoke access anytime in macOS Settings.

Children

Not directed to children under 13.
