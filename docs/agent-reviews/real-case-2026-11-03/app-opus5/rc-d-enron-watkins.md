## Conclusion

1. **Who raised them: Sherron S. Watkins, VP Corporate Development** (high confidence).
   - message-019.eml (dan.dorland@enron.com → chris.dorland@enron.com, 16 Jan 2002, "FW: Text of Letter
     to Enron's Chairman After Departure of Chief Executive"), forwarding the NYT text: "Following is the
     text of an unsigned letter written in August to Kenneth L. Lay … after Jeffrey K. Skilling resigned
     unexpectedly as chief executive on Aug. 14. Its author was later identified as Sherron S. Watkins, a
     vice president for corporate development at Enron." (r5 q07, r4 q17, r4 q01).
   - Corroborated in her own mail: message-056.eml, sherron.watkins@enron.com → rick.buy@enron.com,
     "RE: portrac", Tue 14 Aug 2001 15:27 PDT: "I sure hope we make good use of the bad news about
     Skilling's resignation and do some house cleaning - can we write down some problem assets and unwind
     raptor? I've been horribly uncomfortable about some of our accounting in the past few years … I'm
     concerned some disgruntled employee will tattle. Can you influence some sanity?" (r4 q02/q03).
   - The letter itself (as reprinted) is unsigned; attribution rests on the NYT statement, a secondary
     source forwarded months later. Her own 14 Aug e-mail and the 30 Oct "key questions" e-mail
     independently tie her to the concerns.
2. **What the concerns were** (medium-high; only fragments retrievable). From the letter in message-019:
   Raptor vehicles' accounting "looks questionable" ("Summary of raptor oddities: 1. The accounting
   treatment looks questionable", r2 q02); Raptor entity "capitalized with LJM equity" (r1 q02/q07 —
   note the app attributed this fragment to message-013, wrongly — see log); to avoid a Q1-2001
   write-down "we 'enhanced' the capital structure of the Raptor vehicles, committing more ENE shares",
   and "In the third quarter we have a $250 million problem with Raptor 3 (NPW) if we don't 'enhance'
   the capital structure" (r1 q15, r2 q05); a related party entity "has lost $500 million" (r2 q08,
   r4 q08); "it looks like they booked a $500 million gain from this related party company" (r1 q07
   passage); "Jeff McMahon was highly vexed over the inherent conflicts of LJM. He complained mightily to
   Jeff Skilling and laid out five steps…" (r1 q09 passage); "I am incredibly nervous that we will
   implode in a wave of accounting scandals" (r1 q11 passage); the man-in-the-street disclosure test
   and Waste Management/AA precedent (r1 q01). Recommended: "Law firm to hire one of the big 6, but not
   Arthur Andersen or PricewaterhouseCoopers due to their conflicts of interest" (r1 q12), investigate
   transactions and future commitments (r2 q05), Lay to judge "the probabilities of discovery … and the
   estimated damages" (r5 q11), "Personnel to quiz confidentially to determine if I'm all wet: a. Jeff
   McMahon b. Mark Koenig c. Rick Buy d. Greg Walley" (r1 q15 passage).
3. **When Lay learned: by 22 August 2001 in person** (high for the meeting; letter delivery date
   not fixed). message-005.eml, sherron.watkins@enron.com → e..haedicke@enron.com and
   legal <.schuler@enron.com>, "The key questions I asked Lay on Aug 22", Tue 30 Oct 2001: "I forgot to
   print out this page, this is the page I reviewed with Lay on 8/22." (r3 q05, r2 q09). The NYT text
   says only "written in August … after … Aug. 14". The exact day the letter reached Lay is NOT in the
   messages the app showed me (r5 q08 answered "Published: May 24, 1996." — junk).
4. **Who else knew, and when.**
   - Rick Buy (Chief Risk Officer by role, my knowledge — not stated in the app answers): told on
     14 Aug 2001 by message-056 (above). The "RE:" subject and "Wow - you are really busy if your lunch
     calendar is booked" imply he replied, but his reply was not surfaced (r5 q12 off-target).
   - Mark Haedicke and (legal) Schuler: received message-005 on 30 Oct 2001 (Enron legal by address).
   - Andrew Fastow, Greg Whalley, J. Kean, Mark Koenig, James Derrick, Paula Rieker, Ken Lay: on the
     27 Sep 2001 "LJM Statement" draft from Mark Palmer (message-032) — shows LJM was a press issue by
     then, NOT that they knew of Watkins' concerns.
   - The letter itself lists McMahon, Koenig, Buy and Whalley as people to quiz; that proves she
     *named* them, not that they were told.
   - Cindy Olson forwarded to Lay "FW: Your new CFO spot and the job I want" on 24 Oct 2001 ("Ken per my
     voice mail......thanks.", message-015). The forwarded original was not surfaced; I suspect (memory)
     it is Watkins asking for the CFO role after Fastow's exit — UNVERIFIED in the app.

**Not supported by the messages (as surfaced):** that Lay acted on the concerns; that Vinson & Elkins
