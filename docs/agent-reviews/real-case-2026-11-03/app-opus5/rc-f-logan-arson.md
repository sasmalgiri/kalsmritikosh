## Conclusion

**Planning.** James Gregory Glick (44, Logan) planned the burning to collect the insurance:
- He "arranged to have an office building located at 111 Stratton Street in Logan burned to collect more than $1
  million in insurance proceeds". The indictment dates this to November 2011 (r1 q02).
- The building was a "former law office building" (r1 q15).
- Glick also operated the neighbouring 317 Steakhouse (r1 q13).
- He was out of town on the night: "While Glick was out of town during the early morning hours of February 1, 2012,
  Miller orchestrated the burning…" (r1 q09 passage).

Confidence: high.

## Contradictions between sources

1. **Thompson's payment.** "approximately $75,000" (September 3, 2013 release, r1 q04) against "approximately $50,000"
   (November 6, 2013 release, r4 q08). **The app did not flag it.** Asked "How much did the agent receive, $50,000 or
   $75,000?", it answered "Your archive carries conflicting values for amount: • $50,000 • $50 • $10,000" (r5 q10).
   The real conflict, $75,000, is missing from that list, and unrelated amounts are mixed in.
2. **Who set the fire.**
   - November 2013 release: "Guy Miller, Shawn C. Simon, 41, of Charleston and another associate worked together to set
     the fire by spreading gasoline throughout the main floor" (r1 q07).
   - May 2014 releases: Williams spread the gasoline and lit it; Simon drove the getaway car (r2 q09/q10).
   - The timeline also carries a third variant: "Simon … and Michael Williams … worked together to set the fire…"
     (r2 q14).
   - **Not flagged.** The app's timeline lists two February 1, 2012 entries side by side without comment.
3. **Thompson's plea date.** Expected "Thursday, Nov. 7, 2013" (r5 q04), but actually entered December 12, 2013 (r2
   q04). This is a schedule change, not necessarily an error. Not flagged.
4. **Glick's sentencing date.** "February 19, 2014" in the November 2013 release (r4 q11), but he was sentenced in May
   2014. Postponed; the records give no reason. Not flagged.
5. Minor: Thompson's age is 44 in 2013 and 45 in 2014, consistent with ageing.

The case workflow reported "contradictions found: 0" in every round.

**Effort.** 5 rounds, 83 questions, about 2 minutes of app time per round. Roughly half the answers were directly
usable. I built the role table myself from about 15 good verbatim sentences, and found both contradictions by
comparing quotes by hand.

**Would I rely on it?** As a **sentence finder with citations, yes**, for "who did X" questions whose answer is one
sentence. The per-person "case against X, in date order" view is useful.

**No** for:
- money questions;
- "when" questions;
- contradictions;
- people with sparse coverage (Williams), where it borrowed a neighbour's sentence;
- the findings report: it names the prosecutor as a defendant, shifts dates a day, and reports false gaps.

Every answer must be checked against its quoted passage. The 0.8 "Quoted verbatim" label does not mean the quote
answers the question.
