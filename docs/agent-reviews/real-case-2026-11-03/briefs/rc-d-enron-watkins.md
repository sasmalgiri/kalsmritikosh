# Case rc-d-enron-watkins — briefing

**Question.** In August 2001, after Jeffrey Skilling resigned as Enron's chief executive, an Enron employee raised concerns about the company's accounting. **Who raised them, what exactly were the concerns, when did Chairman Kenneth Lay learn of them, and who else at Enron knew — and when?**

**Your task.** Answer each part with the specific message (sender, recipients, date) that supports it, give a dated timeline, and say which conclusions the messages do not support.

**The archive.** 60 e-mail messages (.eml, as produced) from four Enron mailboxes, August 2001 – February 2002. Most messages are routine business and unrelated to the question.
