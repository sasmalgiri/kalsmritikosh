# Case rc-c-ntsb-moncks-corner — briefing

**Event.** On July 7, 2015, a civilian Cessna 150M and a U.S. Air Force F-16 collided in midair near Moncks Corner, South Carolina.

**Your task.** Reconstruct what happened — the two flights, the air traffic control exchanges, the sequence and timing up to the collision — and assess the likely cause and any contributing factors. Distinguish what the records state from your own assessment.

**The archive.** 3 documents from the NTSB investigation file: a coded record page (text), the factual narrative (PDF) and the analysis (text). The file is as an investigator holds it before the final finding.
