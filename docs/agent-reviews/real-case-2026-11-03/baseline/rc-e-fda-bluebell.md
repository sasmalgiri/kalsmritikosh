## Discrepancies / cautions
- The recall *reason* in 70738 describes the illness cluster ("Five patients … in Kansas"), not a contamination finding at a plant. The record does not say which plants (Brenham TX, Broken Arrow OK, Sylacauga AL) were implicated. Brenham appears only as the firm address and on product sheets; Sylacauga appears only in the 2019 record.
- The 70738 record carries a single initiation date (Mar 13, 2015) for all 286 products. Several early records list specific lot codes ("All lots within expiry including 120816A …") while most say "all lot codes within expiry". The record does not show whether the recall was expanded in stages or when. (General knowledge says it grew in stages up to April 2015; the record does not show this.)
- About three months passed between initiation and classification (Mar 13 → Jun 19, 2015).
- Quantity formatting is inconsistent ("876, 648 units", "248,526 uniits", four blanks), and the distribution list has typos ("Saudia Arabia", "Trinadad"). An exact total is impossible to state, so the about 477M figure is approximate.
- Event 75254 started while 70738 was still open. It is a separate event ID with a separately named source (Aspen Hills), so it must not be merged with the 2015 event.

## What the records do not show
- Deaths, the number of illnesses beyond the five Kansas patients, or the states of the patients.
- Plant-level findings, FDA inspection observations, CDC involvement, production shutdowns or restarts.
- Any enforcement action beyond the recall.
- Whether Aspen Hills issued its own recall.
- How Blue Bell's 2015 contamination arose.
- Any link between the Jeni's and Blue Bell contamination.
