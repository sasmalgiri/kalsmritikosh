**The event.** The fire was set on the night / early hours of Feb 1, 2012 at 111 Stratton Street, downtown Logan, a "former law office building" (R5) across the street from Glick's 317 Steakhouse (R4).

## Where the records disagree
1. **Who spread the fuel.** R2: "Guy Miller, Shawn C. Simon, 41, of Charleston and another associate worked together to set the fire by spreading gasoline throughout the main floor." R3 says the same with Williams named. R4/R5: **Williams** alone spread about 15 gallons; Simon drove the getaway car; Miller recruited and orchestrated.
2. **Which camera filmed it.** R4: "The three culprits were caught on 317’s security camera". R5: "The explosion was caught on the Logan County Courthouse video security system" *and* "The Main Street side security cameras for the restaurant captured Miller, Simon and Williams fleeing the scene". R3: Simon destroyed "a security camera"; R4/R5 say the "digital video recorder".
3. **Who destroyed the evidence.** R4: "Glick had Miller and Simon destroy the restaurant’s digital video recorder". R5 and R3: Simon alone ("Simon admitted that he destroyed the video recorder … and threw it into the Kanawha River").
4. **Payment to Thompson.** All four readable releases say about **$50,000**. The README's "$75k" cannot be checked: it is not in R2–R5, and R1 is unreadable.
5. **Purchase price and buyer.** R2/R3: a known person bought the building for $45,000 and resold it to Glick "purportedly for $50,000". R4/R5: "Glick purchased … for $50,000".
6. **Who planned it.** R2/R3: "Glick arranged". R5: "initially hatched by Glick and another co-conspirator" (unnamed).
7. **Sentencing day for Glick and Thompson.** R4 is dated Thursday May 22, 2014 and says "sentenced in federal court yesterday" (May 21). Its last line says Judge Johnston "imposed today’s sentences". R5 (May 30) says "Last week Glick received a sentence".
8. **$3,900 restitution.** R4: "the Court also ordered Glick and Thompson to reimburse the City of Logan $3,900". R5: "ordered all defendants".
9. **Thompson's exposure.** R3: "faces up to faces a minimum of seven years" (garbled), but his conviction was a conspiracy count.
10. **Simon's plea.** R3 says obstruction of justice. R4 generalises that the conspirators "ultimately pled guilty to their respective roles in the conspiracy to commit arson and to defraud General Star".
11. **"25 Years Of Prison Time"** (R5 headline). The five sentences total 87 + 60 + 75 + 32 + 22 = 276 months (23 years). Only with Workman's 27 months (303 months, about 25.25 years) does it reach 25. So the headline counts Workman.
12. **Ages and names.** Miller is 39 (R2, R3) then 40 and "Guy R. Miller, Jr." (R4, R5). Thompson 44 → 45. Glick 44 throughout. The fire is on "the night of February 1" (R2, R3) vs "the early hours of February 1" (R4, R5).
13. **Investigating agencies.** These differ by release: WVSP and IRS (R2, R4); plus the WV Insurance Commission (R3); plus the State Fire Marshal (R5).
