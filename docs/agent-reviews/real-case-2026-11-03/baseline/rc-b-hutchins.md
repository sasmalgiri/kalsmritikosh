## Which documents are about John L. Hutchins
- **About him:** R2, R3, R5, R8. Each names "John L. Hutchins … of Lewiston, NY" and the Rapids Theatre.
- **Not about him:**
  - R1 is **William Hutchins**, 37, of North Tonawanda: cocaine conspiracy and car arson, 37 months, Judge Arcara, 2014.
  - R4, R6 and R7 are **Patricia Hutchins**, 53, of Brockport: PPP/unemployment/elder fraud and Kenny Chesney excuse, 16 months, Judge Geraci, Rochester.
  - These are the same surname, the same district, and (for Patricia) a PPP-fraud theme too, but they are different people. None of these documents links them to John L. Hutchins.
- One trap: R2/R3 say the loan money paid for "residential properties in North Tonawanda, NY, and Lewiston, NY". North Tonawanda is also William Hutchins's town (R1). That does not connect the two men.

## Contradictions / discrepancies
1. **EIDL loans.** R2/R3: "four Economic Injury Disaster Loans totaling $749,500.00". R5: "five … totaling $779,500.00". R8: $779,500.00, no count given.
2. **PPP loans.** R2: "a Paycheck Protection Program loan totaling $74,838". R3/R8: "two … totaling $115,978". R5 splits them: Bank on Buffalo $74,838 plus Northwest Bank $41,140, which equals $115,978.
3. **Total.** R2/R3 headline "more than $750,000" (before SVOG). R5/R8 "more than $1.8 million". The R8 IRS quote says "nearly $2 million".
4. **SVOG timing.** R8 says all three loan types, SVOG included, were received "Between March and August 2020". R5 gives that window for the EIDLs only and a conspiracy running to March 2024. I treat R8's sentence as a probable drafting compression.
5. **Soliman's age.** 39 (Mar 2022), 40 (Sept 2024), 45 (Jan 2026). That is +1 in 2.5 years, then +5 in 16 months, so the ages are inconsistent. Hutchins's ages (68 → 70 → 71 → 72) are consistent.
6. **Soliman's laundering count.** "money laundering" (R2) became "engaging in monetary transactions with criminally derived property" (R3).
7. **Hutchins's false-statement count.** Charged in R2/R3, but the plea (R5) and sentence (R8) cover only the conspiracy. The record does not say whether that count was dismissed.
8. **Name.** "Rapids Theater" in the quoted false statement vs "Rapids Theatre" elsewhere. This is cosmetic.
9. **Patricia Hutchins (look-alike), internal:** search at "Hutchins’ Rochester" (R4) vs "Greece residence" (R6, R7); restitution "approximately $60,000" (R6) vs "approximately $65,000" (R7). These are noted only so they are not attributed to John L. Hutchins.
