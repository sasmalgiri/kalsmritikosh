## Contradictions

**Flagged by the app:**
1. Soliman's age, 39 (Mar 2022) vs 40 (Sep 2024) vs 45 (Jan 2026). This is **real and correctly flagged** (rules; all three pairs). Elapsed time cannot explain it: someone who is 39 in March 2022 is 41–42 by September 2024 and 42–43 by January 2026. Hutchins's own ages (68, 70, 71, 72) are consistent.
2. EIDL count and total, four loans / $749,500 (2022, 2024) vs five / $779,500 (2025, 2026); PPP one loan / $74,838 (2022) vs two / $115,978 (2024 onward). The quotes are **real differences**, but they read as the charges growing: the superseding indictment and plea added a loan each. Release-05's two PPP loans ($74,838 + $41,140) add up exactly to $115,978. The app frames them as "which account is right is not established", which overstates the conflict.
3. "July 2021 search warrant … Greece residence vs Rochester". This is about **Patricia** Hutchins, not John. It is also probably not a conflict, because the Rochester quote is truncated ("Hutchins' Rochester…") and Greece is a place name. It is **misleading** in a case about John L. Hutchins because the label just says "Hutchins' residence".
4. In-case timeline "Alternative accounts: Conflicting dates for 'John L. Hutchins charged' — Mar 29, 2022 / Sep 12, 2024". **Wrong.** These are the indictment and the superseding indictment, not two dates for one event.

**Found by me:** release-08 says both men "were convicted", while release-05 records Hutchins's guilty plea. This is consistent (a plea is a conviction) but worth noting. Soliman's route to conviction is not recorded.
