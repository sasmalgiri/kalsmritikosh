## Contradictions

App-flagged (r1: 11; 5 rules + 6 AI-verified):
1. **Thompson's fee: $50,000 vs ~$75,000.** Real. release-01 (indictment release) says ~$75,000. releases 02, 03 and 04 say ~$50,000 / "$50k". Flagged 4 times as near-duplicates (#4, #6, #8, #9). Unresolved by the record.
2. **Simon's role: spread gasoline vs drove the getaway car.** Real. release-03 says Miller, Simon and Williams "worked together to set the fire … by spreading gasoline". release-05 says Simon "drove the getaway car". Flagged twice (#5, #11).
3. **Who spread the fuel: Miller/Simon/"another person" vs Williams.** Real but explainable. The early releases withhold the third person's name, and the later ones name Williams as the one who spread fuel. Flagged 3 times (#2, #3, #10).
4. **317's security camera vs Logan County Courthouse video.** I judge this a false positive. These are two different recordings: the restaurant camera "caught the three culprits", while the courthouse system "caught the explosion" (r3 q05). They don't conflict.
5. **Miller sentencing Feb 19, 2014 vs May 28, 2014.** False positive. The first is a scheduled date that was later reset. The app's own dropped-pairs list correctly dismissed the same pattern for Simon (Feb 20 vs May 29), but kept Miller's.

Found by me, not flagged as contradictions:
- The investigating agencies differ by release: IRS + State Police (release-02); + Insurance Commission (release-03); State Police + Fire Marshal + IRS-CI (release-05). These are omissions, not conflicts.
- Ages 39→40 (Miller) and 44→45 (Thompson). The app's q15 answer offered these as disagreements. They are explained by elapsed time, so not contradictions.
- release-03 says "Thompson faces up to faces a minimum of seven years". That is a typo in the source.
