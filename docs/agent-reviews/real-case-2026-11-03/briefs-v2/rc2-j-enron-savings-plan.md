# Case rc2-j-enron-savings-plan — briefing

**Question.** In autumn 2001, as Enron's stock fell, employees were for a time unable to move money held in the company's 401(k) Savings Plan. **What restriction applied, from when to when, and why? What were employees told, by whom and when — and when did the restriction actually end? Which individual employees in these mailboxes were affected or raised concerns, what happened to them, and was it resolved?**

**Your task.** Answer each part with the specific message (sender, recipients, date) that supports it, give a dated timeline, and say which conclusions the messages do not support.

**The archive.** 62 e-mail messages (.eml, as produced) from four Enron mailboxes, June 2000 – February 2002. Most messages are routine business and unrelated to the question.
