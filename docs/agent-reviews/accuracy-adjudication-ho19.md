## Method

Same method and judgement rules as ho14–ho18 (`accuracy-adjudication-ho14.md` … `-ho18.md`). Work files: `~/CaseBench/adjudication/ho19-work` (`flat.py`, `fam.py`, `g.py`, `verdict.py`, extracted texts under `text/`). New this time: a small harness (`/tmp/ho19h`, the unmodified `ProceedingAnswerer.swift`, `ProceedingEventExtractor.swift` and `IssuingOfficeGate.swift` of 6a029132 compiled with stubs for the answer type) reproduced every root cause below on the release texts and question wordings.

1. **Blind first.** I read all 47 scorer-flagged items (rules 27, opus5 20) with their questions and answers, and judged them against the case documents before reading the keys' expected values. After that I used the keys only to map items to cases and to name key defects.
2. **Documents.** All 135 case documents were extracted: PDFKit for the 69 PDFs, `textutil` for doc/docx/rtf/html, plain text as is. Five scanned PDFs (Brandow ×2, Kelerchian ×3) were read with Apple Vision OCR (lossy: some lines drop; where the app quoted a line my OCR lost, the app's verbatim quote was accepted only when it is consistent with the rest of the document). No byte-identical files (MD5).
3. **Verdicts.** TRUE WRONG, SCORER ERROR, TRUTH-LABEL ERROR, PARTLY, AMBIGUOUS, MISSED, FALSE CORRECT. The judgement rules, unchanged:
   - **PARTLY** = the main claim is true but incomplete; or a secondary element or row is misdated or mislabelled; or a row or second value belongs to another case or person. Not counted in the primary measure; counted in the strict measure.
   - **"Charged with" naming no offence = WRONG**. An offence named only vaguely, anaphorically or as its conduct = PARTLY (Hodges ho15, Farnell ho16, Burns ho18).
   - **Another (prior, separate or co-defendant's) case's or person's step given as this person's = WRONG**; the same as a second value or extra row next to the right one = PARTLY.
   - **A timeline is WRONG** when its rows put the case in an impossible order; a misdated, mislabelled or missing row with the order still possible = PARTLY.
   - **A non-answer** (a question answered with a sentence that does not answer it) = WRONG.
   - **Office**: the investigating/issuing component alone is minor CORRECT when the documents name no prosecuting district (Hayes, Richardson ho17); WRONG when they do (Bounds ho18). A right office plus a spurious second office = PARTLY.
   - "It gives no other day" where the release's own "today" is the only day is minor CORRECT; where the release states another day for the step ("yesterday", "on July 21") it is PARTLY (Estrada ho18, Gray/Blackhair ho16).
   - Report-date-hedged rows that are otherwise true, "who was convicted of …, was sentenced" (and "who pled guilty …, received a sentence today") rows dated on the sentencing day, and hedged prior-case rows are minor and recorded CORRECT.
   - **MISSED** (a wrong refusal) is reported separately (ho14/ho16 convention) with a strict + missed column.
4. **Search for false "correct" verdicts — a full check, not a sample.** Every answer on both paths in *charge* (208), *sentenced* (240), *charged* (96), *timeline* (240), *sentence* (144) and *plea* (200). Each stated day was checked against the release date and against the "today / yesterday / weekday / on <date>" of its source sentence; each timeline row for date, label, order, completeness and whose event it is. Every *count* answer (240) was checked against the number of the case's releases, and every *office* answer (240) against the releases.
5. **The rest.** A seeded random sample of 40 from the *count*, *office* and *absent* items scored correct or honest refusal (20 rules, seed 20261106; 20 opus5, seeds 20261106 and 20261107 — the first seed drew the same items on both paths, so 12 further opus5 items were drawn).
6. **Counting.** Wrong % counts dumps and unsupported answers as wrong when they are wrong (none were). There were no AMBIGUOUS items.
