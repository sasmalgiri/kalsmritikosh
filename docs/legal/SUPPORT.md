## What Kalsmritikosh is
A private, on-device app that turns a folder of your documents and emails into a
searchable evidence ledger and reconstructed timeline, and answers questions with
citations you can open. Everything runs on your Mac; nothing is uploaded.

## Requirements
- macOS 15.6 or later
- Apple Intelligence enabled — the app uses Apple's on-device model to write answers.
  You can still ingest, browse, search, and view evidence without it; only the
  AI-written answers require it.

## Common questions
- **Does my data leave my Mac?** No — the app runs fully on-device. Nothing is
  uploaded; there is no account and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.
- **Why is an answer "unsupported" or refused?** The app answers only from your
  documents; if the evidence isn't there, it says so rather than guessing.
- **Can I correct a fact?** Yes — reviews are recorded without deleting the original
  evidence.
- **How do I delete everything?** Settings → Data → Delete, or remove the app.
- **Which file types are supported?** See the in-app onboarding / SUPPORTED_FORMATS_V1.

## Reporting a problem
Email the above with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, and (optionally)
an exported diagnostics file (Settings → Diagnostics). Diagnostics may contain file
paths/content, so review before sending — it is never sent automatically.

## Accuracy
Kalsmritikosh assists analysis; it is not legal/financial/professional advice and
makes no admissibility claim. Verify material facts independently. See the EULA.
