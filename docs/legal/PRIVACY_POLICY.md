## The short version

Kalsmritikosh is a private, on-device application. **Your documents, the knowledge
built from them, your questions, and the answers never leave your Mac.** We do not
operate servers that receive your content. We do not have accounts, tracking, ads,
analytics, or telemetry.

## What the app processes, and where

- **Your files:** You select folders/files. The app reads them **in place** on your
  Mac to build a local index (a single on-device database). Files are not copied to
  us or anyone else.
- **Derived data** (text excerpts, entities, dates, relationships, embeddings,
  summaries, answers) is stored **only** in the app's local database inside your
  user Library container. Deleting the app or using in-app "Delete data" removes it.
- **AI models run on-device.** Reasoning and embedding models execute locally; your
  content is never sent to a cloud model. The v1 release contains **no cloud
  reasoning path.**

## Network use

The app is designed to work offline. Network access is used **only** for:

1. **Optional model download** — if you choose to download the larger optional model
   on a capable Mac, the app downloads model weights from [MODEL HOST URL]. No
   document content is transmitted; only the model file is fetched.
2. **Opening a source** in another app, or links you click, use the system normally.

No document text, questions, answers, filenames, or usage data are transmitted for
any purpose. There is no analytics or crash-telemetry SDK.

## Data you export

Any export you trigger (answers, reports, diagnostics) is written to a location you
choose or an app-private folder. Exports may contain your source content and file
paths — they are created only on your explicit action and are never uploaded.

## Permissions

- **Files & folders:** to read the archive you choose (macOS security-scoped
  bookmarks). You can revoke access anytime in macOS Settings.
