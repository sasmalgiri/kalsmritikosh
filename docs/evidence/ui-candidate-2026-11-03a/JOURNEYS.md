## Other observations

- **John Carter not linked.** The case's existing person "John Carter" shows "Link to…" instead of "Linked", although its menu offers exactly one match, "John Carter (Person)". The newly added Maria Lopez linked at once (F1-people-tree.txt l.54–55, F3-added-tree.txt).
- **Undated entries show a date.** Chronology entries added without a date show "3 Nov 2026", the day they were added, in the date column of the Chronology tab. The case file report prints "—" for the same entries.
- **Internal ids instead of file names.** Search hits name their document as "KO B96A667D · chunk #0" rather than the file name. The Tag sheet says "Document ABB0B3EA". The chronology "Open source" sheet says "Source KO: B96A667D".
- **Highlight colour in Ask.** The Ask cited-passage sheet now marks the quote with a grey selection tint, and focus sits in the new highlight field. The earlier passes showed a yellow highlight (02c A3-source-sheet.png).
- **Modules missing for an existing investigator.** "Watchlists", "Who talks to whom" and "Timeline and relationship views" were OFF although the persona is Investigator. The investigation preset in ModulePreset lists them, but the preset is applied only when a role is chosen. An existing investigator does not see the new Watchlists tool until they choose the role again or switch the module on.
- **Duplicate case names.** "Project Delta slip" appears twice in the case lists of both the chronology sheet and the Watchlists scope menu.
- **Timeline extraction.** The export carries "Invoice issued … on Jun 14, 2024 … invoice-432.eml", while the email says the invoice was issued 2024-05-30. This is an extraction or date question, not an export defect.
