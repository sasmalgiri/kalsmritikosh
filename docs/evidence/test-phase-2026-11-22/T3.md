## Defects found and fixed in the code

| Where | Failure | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| `HistoryArtifactRepository.save` (HISTORY-09, L3 supersede) | After the ledger changed, a story rebuild saved a header with no items. It returned nil, was never superseded, and "what changed" compared against the empty header ("5 new" for 1 new). | History item ids are stable across rebuilds because the engine derives them. `history_items.id` is the primary key, so the first item that was kept failed the insert. There was no transaction. | The whole save runs in one savepoint. A kept item or gap gets its own row id in the new artifact, and the earlier artifact keeps its row. |
| `AppState.composeStoryAnswer` | A failed save still read "This story was saved to your archive." | The wording did not check `artifactID`. | It now says "saved" only when the story was saved. |
| `DiagnosticsSanitizer` (App/Diagnostics) | The test host crashed (EXC_BAD_ACCESS) while saving a MetricKit diagnostic payload. This took down 5 tests in one run. | MetricKit call-stack trees nest one level per frame. `JSONSerialization` writing them back on MetricKit's delivery thread overflowed that thread's stack. In the app, this crashes while it saves its own crash report. | Nesting below 160 levels is replaced by "[deeper levels removed]". Regression test: `UnifiedPathRound2Tests/deepDiagnosticPayloadIsSaved()`. |
| `FactValueNormalizer` (IN-27) | `"<mailto:Asha.Rao@Example.com>."` became `mailto:asha.rao@example.com>` | `mailto:` was looked for before the wrapper was trimmed, and `>` was not treated as trailing. | The wrapper is trimmed first, and closing brackets and quotes count as trailing. |
| `CaseBatchJournal` (AI-39, testability) | `batchJournal` failed only in company. | Every AppState booted by another suite replaced the process-wide journal. | Added a DEBUG task-local `TestOverride.journal`. Release behaviour is unchanged. |

## Tests that were wrong at first (the test was fixed)

- `SmartMapAcceptanceJourneyTests/decisiveJourney`:
  - It failed once in company with `encryptedWithAnotherKey` and passed alone. The test host's in-memory key store is process-wide, and a concurrent recovery-key or restore suite stores another key.
  - The journey now restores and reopens with the ledger's own key.
- `briefingEverySource`: `claim_evidence_ref` (rebuilt by a later migration) needs `ordinal` and `evidence_role`. The shelf header itself contains "[D<n>]".
- `shardFoldVectorsRegisters`: a tombstone needs its generation row. The merge re-numbers it correctly.
- Story tests on a booted app:
  - Seeded events must carry the current `producer_version`, or the background drain replaces them.
  - An identifier anchor needs a fact naming it, or the orphan-anchor sweep removes it.
- `mergedMultiSubjectStory`: chapters (one subject and year each) are merged by their first date, as documented. Items are not interleaved across chapters.
- `storyRewordingAndWhatChanged`: after a relaunch, the app's own upkeep may rebuild the story first. The comparison then correctly says "nothing changed" since that save. Both outcomes are accepted, and the test also checks that every saved history has its items.

No privacy, consent or erasure assertion was weakened.
