Outputs may be incomplete, out of date, or wrong. The App distinguishes direct evidence from assertion and inference and shows citations, but:

  • it is not legal, financial, medical, or professional advice;
  • it makes no claim of legal admissibility or forensic soundness;
  • "confidence", "verified", and evidence labels are software heuristics, not guarantees of truth;
  • AI transcriptions, translations, challenges and drafts are produced by an AI model, are marked as such, and are not the original document, recording or a finding;
  • you must independently verify any material fact before relying on it.

The App is an aid for investigators. It checks each AI statement against the source passage it cites before showing it, and leaves out statements it cannot match; that check reduces errors but does not remove them, and a statement can be accurately quoted and still be incomplete or misleading in context. There is no guarantee of accuracy. The App does not decide findings, guilt, credibility or outcomes — every conclusion is the investigator's.
