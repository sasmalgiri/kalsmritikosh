# Amendment No. 7 — Project Delta Master Supply Agreement

**Effective Date:** May 02, 2024
**Parties:** Supplier ABC and Northwind Industries.

This amendment to the Project Delta supply agreement (signed January 12, 2024) is a contract modified record. Approval of this amendment was delayed by 21 days due to internal procurement review at Northwind Industries, contributing to overall schedule slippage on Project Delta.

## Changes

1. The delivery date for the first lot of 8 turbine blade assemblies is **rescheduled from 2024-04-15 to 2024-05-28** due to material certification delays cited by Supplier ABC.
2. The delivery date for the remaining 16 units is **rescheduled from 2024-06-30 to 2024-08-15**.
3. Liquidated damages of USD 25,000 per week of further delay are waived for the first lot only.

Signed by John Carter (Northwind) and Maria Lopez (Supplier ABC) on May 2, 2024.
