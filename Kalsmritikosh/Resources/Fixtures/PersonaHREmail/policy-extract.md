# Northgate Logistics — Flexible Rostering Policy (extract)

**Section 4.2 — Notice of roster changes.** Employees must receive at least
24 hours' notice of any roster change, except in a declared operational
emergency recorded by the duty manager.

**Section 4.3 — Review.** An employee may request a rostering review; HR
must complete it within 15 working days.

Policy effective 1 January 2025. Owner: People Operations.
