# FOIA response — Ref FOI-2025-0611

Date: 22 July 2025
From: Records Office, Harbor City Transit Authority

Your request for maintenance logs of the Line 3 signal system (January to
June 2025) is granted in part. Released: 214 pages of logs. Withheld: 12
pages under exemption 7(b) (ongoing investigation).

The released logs record 9 signal fault events on Line 3 between January
and June 2025, of which 4 occurred at the Weststation junction.
