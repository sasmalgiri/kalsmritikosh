Neha Sharma and Amandeep Sharma
Serious Fraud Office (New Zealand) — case page (closed | Case number: 221236)

Former Oranga Tamariki employee Neha Sharma and her husband Amandeep Sharma have admitted to working together to fraudulently obtain $2 million from Oranga Tamariki.

Case Timeline

- Jun 2023

Amandeep Sharma appears in Christchurch District Court facing charges of obtaining by deception and money laundering. Neha Sharma is in India and a warrant has been issued for her arrest, however it will lie in court until the defendants' next scheduled appearance on 26 July 2023.

- Sept 2023

Neha and Amandeep Sharma appear in the Christchurch District Court and plead not guilty to all charges.

- Sept 2024

Trial scheduled to commence 8 September 2025

- Mar 2025

Neha Sharma pleads guilty to charges of obtaining by deception and using a forged document.

- May 2025

Neha Sharma is sentenced to three years' imprisonment.

- May 2025

Amandeep Sharma pleads guilty to charges of obtaining by deception.

- Jun 2025

Amandeep Sharma sentenced in the Christchurch High Court to 12 months’ home detention.
