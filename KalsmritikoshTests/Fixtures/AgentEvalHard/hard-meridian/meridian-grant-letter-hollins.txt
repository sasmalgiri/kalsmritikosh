Dear Trustees,

Grant award HF/2026/118 — "Every Child a Reader" programme

I am pleased to confirm that the Foundation's grants committee has awarded the Meridian Children's Literacy Trust a grant of £2.4 million for the "Every Child a Reader" programme in North Yorkshire primary schools.

The grant is restricted to the purchase and distribution of reading kits and the training of volunteer reading partners. The full amount will be paid in a single transfer within seven days of this letter. Any goods purchased with the grant must be delivered before payment is released to the supplier, and the Trust must keep delivery records for our audit.

Please send a progress report by 30 September 2026.

Yours sincerely,

Margaret Ellery
Director of Grants, The Hollins Foundation
