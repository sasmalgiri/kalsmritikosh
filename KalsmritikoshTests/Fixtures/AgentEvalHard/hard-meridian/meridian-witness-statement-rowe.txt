STATEMENT OF WITNESS

Name: Thomas Rowe
Occupation: Volunteer warehouse assistant, Meridian Children's Literacy Trust
Date: 1 July 2026
Interviewed by: K. Lambert, Charity Commission investigator

I volunteer at the Beck Mill store on Saturdays. On Saturday 9 May 2026 a van from Larkfield came in the morning. It was a big delivery, about forty boxes of reading kits, and it filled the back bay. I signed for it in the goods-in book. I did not open the boxes. The driver did not leave a delivery note.

I have not seen any other delivery from Larkfield.

Signed: Tom Rowe
