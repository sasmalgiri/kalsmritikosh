NORTHGATE PORT AUTHORITY
MINUTES OF THE BOARD MEETING HELD ON THURSDAY 5 FEBRUARY 2026

Present: Chair Helen Brandt; members J. Osei, P. Lindqvist, R. Moreau; Daniel Okafor (Procurement Director, presenting); Ruth Adeyemi (Senior Port Engineer, in attendance).

Item 4 — Award of the Berths 4–7 maintenance dredging contract

Mr Okafor reported that three bids had been received and that the bid scoring meeting was held on Monday 2 February 2026. He recommended award to Deepline Dredging Inc. at $2.4 million. The next-ranked bid, from Coastline Works Ltd, was $2.15 million but, in Mr Okafor's words, "scored lower on equipment availability".

Mr Lindqvist asked why the lowest bid was not preferred. Mr Okafor replied that the scoring panel had been unanimous.

Resolution: the Board approves the award of the contract to Deepline Dredging Inc. for $2.4 million and authorises the Procurement Director to sign it.

Item 5 — Any other business: none.

Minutes approved: H. Brandt, Chair
