WITNESS STATEMENT

Name: Ruth Adeyemi
Position: Senior Port Engineer, Northgate Port Authority
Date of statement: 20 April 2026
Taken by: Investigator S. Whitcombe, Office of the Inspector General

1. I was a member of the scoring panel for the Berths 4–7 dredging tender. The scoring meeting took place on 2 February 2026 in the Authority's second-floor meeting room.

2. Daniel Okafor was present at that meeting. He spoke at length in favour of Deepline Dredging and said that Coastline Works "could not be relied on" for equipment. Coastline's price was the lowest.

3. My own scores placed Coastline first. After Mr Okafor's intervention, the panel's equipment scores for Coastline were lowered and Deepline came out ahead. The panel was not unanimous; I recorded my disagreement in my scoring notes.

4. I did not know of any consultant working for Deepline on this contract. No consultant was disclosed to the panel.

Signed: R. Adeyemi
