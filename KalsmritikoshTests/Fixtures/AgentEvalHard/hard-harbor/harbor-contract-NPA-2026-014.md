# CONTRACT NO. NPA-2026-014 — MAINTENANCE DREDGING, BERTHS 4 TO 7

**Between:** Northgate Port Authority, 1 Harbour Road, Northgate ("the Authority")
**And:** Deepline Dredging Inc., 220 Foundry Street, Northgate ("the Contractor")

**Date of agreement:** 9 February 2026

## 1. Scope
The Contractor shall carry out maintenance dredging of Berths 4 to 7 to a depth of 11.5 metres below chart datum, including disposal of dredged material at the licensed offshore site, in accordance with Schedule A.

## 2. Contract price
The total contract price is USD 2,400,000 (two million four hundred thousand United States dollars), inclusive of all taxes, mobilisation and disposal costs.

## 3. Payment
3.1 A first instalment of USD 1,400,000 is payable on mobilisation of the dredger to site.
3.2 The balance of USD 1,000,000 is payable on completion, after the Authority's hydrographic survey confirms the required depth.
3.3 Payments shall be made by bank transfer to the Contractor's account ending 7731.

## 4. Subcontracting and consultants
The Contractor shall not engage any subcontractor or consultant for this work without the prior written approval of the Authority's Procurement Director. Any consultant engaged must be disclosed to the Authority with its fees.

## 5. Conflicts of interest
The Contractor warrants that no officer or employee of the Authority, or any relative of such a person, has any interest in the Contractor or in any of its subcontractors or consultants.
