# DEPOSIT ESCROW AGREEMENT — VANTAGE QUAY APARTMENTS, GALWAY

**Parties**
1. Corrib Vantage Developments Ltd ("the Developer")
2. Hanley & Moore Solicitors, acting as escrow agent ("the Escrow Agent")
3. The purchasers of apartments 1 to 12, Vantage Quay ("the Purchasers")

**Date:** 2 June 2026

## Clause 1 — Deposits
Each Purchaser pays a booking deposit of EUR 200,000 into the Escrow Agent's client account at Allied Western Bank, account ending 5150 ("the Client Account"). The deposits are held for the Purchasers and are not the property of the Developer or of the Escrow Agent.

## Clause 2 — Release
2.1 No part of the deposits may be released before the architect's certificate of practical completion for the building has been issued and delivered to the Escrow Agent.
2.2 Any release must be instructed in writing and signed by a partner of the Escrow Agent.
2.3 The Escrow Agent shall not pay any deposit money to a party other than the Developer or a Purchaser.

## Clause 3 — Reconciliation
The Escrow Agent shall reconcile the Client Account monthly and shall confirm to the Purchasers' representative that the full deposits are held.

Signed for the Purchasers: Úna Fahy, Purchasers' representative
