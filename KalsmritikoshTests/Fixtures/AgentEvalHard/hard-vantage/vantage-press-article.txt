CONNACHT BUSINESS POST — 14 July 2026

Vantage Quay sells out in five weeks

All twelve apartments at Vantage Quay, the waterfront scheme by Corrib Vantage Developments, have been sold off the plans, the developer said yesterday. Buyers have paid €2.4 million in booking deposits, which are held in escrow by the Galway solicitors Hanley & Moore until the building is complete.

"Every euro of the deposits stays in escrow until the architect signs off," said Gavin Corr, finance director of Corrib Vantage. Completion is expected in spring 2027.

The developer would not comment on reports that construction had paused in June over a dispute with its main contractor.
