HARTWELL LOSS ADJUSTERS — INTERIM REPORT No. 1

To: Southern Cross Mutual Insurance, Claims Department
Insured: Kiln Street Logistics Pty Ltd
Policy: SCM-CP-0098812
Date of report: 27 July 2026
Adjuster: Priya Raman

1. Claim. The insured claims $2.4 million for 60 lots of palletised stock destroyed in the fire of 14 July 2026.

2. Site inspection (20 July). The roof has collapsed over the eastern half. The remains of pallets and racking were counted where accessible. On our count the debris is consistent with roughly 30 to 35 lots, not 60, but the collapsed section could not be entered safely and we draw no conclusion yet.

3. Recommendation. An interim payment of $900,000 on account, without admission of liability, to allow the insured to keep trading, with the balance to await our final report and the fire service's findings on the cause.

4. Further enquiries: stock movements in the two weeks before the fire; any sales of stock; CCTV (the insured says the system was not recording).
