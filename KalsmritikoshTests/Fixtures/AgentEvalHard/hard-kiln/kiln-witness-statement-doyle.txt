WITNESS STATEMENT

Name: Lachlan Doyle
Occupation: Security patrol officer, Eastside Patrols (contracted to the Dandenong South industrial estate)
Date: 3 September 2026
Taken by: J. Whitlam, Southern Cross Mutual investigations

On the night of Sunday 12 July 2026, at about 11:30 pm, on my patrol along Kiln Street, I saw a white rigid truck backed up to the rear gate of number 88. Two men were loading pallets into it with a forklift. The warehouse lights at the east end were on. I could not see the truck's number plate. I did not know the men. I logged it in my patrol book as "loading - 88 Kiln St - 2330" because loading at that hour is unusual.

When I passed again at about 1:15 am the truck was gone and the gate was shut.

Signed: L. Doyle
