Ethical Forestry Limited
Serious Fraud Office — case page (last updated 30 September 2026)

The SFO launched an investigation into suspected fraud at Ethical Forestry Limited, an allegedly fraudulent tree-based investment scheme, in 2017.

On 14 June 2023, the SFO charged former directors of the company, Matthew Pickard, Stephen Greenaway and Paul Laver, with two counts of “Conspiracy to commit fraud by false representation”, contrary to section 1 of the Criminal Law Act 1977 and one count of “Fraudulent trading”, contrary to section 993 of the Companies Act 2006.

On 3 September 2026, Matthew Pickard was sentenced to six years in prison for fraudulent trading. Stephen Greenaway was sentenced to five years and three months. Paul Laver was sentenced to four years and six months. All three were also disqualified from acting as directors for ten years.

This concludes the SFO’s criminal investigation into Ethical Forestry Limited. Our proceeds of crime case continues.

Case timeline

3 September 2026 – Matthew Pickard was sentenced to six years in prison for fraudulent trading at Southwark Crown Court. Stephen Greenaway and Paul Laver were sentenced to five years and three months, and four years and six months respectively in prison for the same offence. Press release.

25 June 2026 – The hearing was adjourned and sentencing is expected for 2-3 September 2026.

16 January 2026 – The defendants pleaded guilty to fraudulent trading.

28 Mar 2025 – All three defendants plead not guilty to all charges at Southwark Crown Court.

26 Jul 2023 – Matthew Pickard, Stephen Greenaway and Paul Laver appeared at Southwark Crown Court for a first hearing. A trial has been listed for early 2026. The SFO will be in contact with individuals who have provided a witness statement in this matter.

28 Jun 2023 – Matthew Pickard, Stephen Greenaway and Paul Laver – attended their first hearing at Westminster Magistrates’ Court.

14 Jun 2023 – Former Directors of Ethical Forestry Limited, namely; Matthew Pickard, Stephen Greenaway and Paul Laver, are charged with the following offences:

- Two counts of “Conspiracy to commit fraud by false representation”, contrary to section 1 of the Criminal Law Act 1977.

- One count of “Fraudulent trading”, contrary to section 993 of the Companies Act 2006.

8 Mar 2017 – SFO investigation announced. The SFO, in conjunction with Dorset Police, has executed search warrants at three addresses.

Information for investors

Investors Questionnaire to Complete

Following on from the three defendants pleading guilty to fraudulent trading, the SFO will now look to recover assets from the convicted defendants. If you are a victim who invested monies into this scheme and wish to be included on the compensation schedule, we invite you to click on the link below and complete the questionnaire by 31 December 2026. Victim’s responses will be collated, and the information will be provided to the judge to consider making a compensation order at a later hearing.

Please be aware that any compensation order the court may make will not recover all of your investment. Any compensation due may well be paid piecemeal as the funds are recovered.

Questionnaire for Ethical Forestry Ltd. Victims

We understand you may already have completed a questionnaire; however this questionnaire is separate and gathers different information, which is why we are inviting you to complete it.

We thank those investors who completed an earlier questionnaire during our investigation and ask that you please retain any correspondence or documents you hold in respect of your investment.

Please do not forget to update us if your contact details change.

Should you have any queries or require assistance completing our questionnaire, please contact us at ethical@sfo.gov.uk who will be happy to assist.
