# App Store Custom Product Pages — copy pack (2026-08-19; R-2 review 2026-10-24)

One app, nine storefronts: create one Custom Product Page per persona in
App Store Connect (Features ▸ Custom Product Pages; limit 70). Each CPP gets
its own URL (use it in that persona's landing page and any ads), its own
promotional text (≤170 chars — lengths verified below), and its own
screenshot ordering. Since July 2025 CPPs can also carry their own keyword
assignments and appear in organic App Store search.

R-2 (App Review 2.3.1, 2026-10-24): every page sells the app as
Investigations Made Easy, keeps judgement with the person (the app organises
and cites; it never proves, decides or verifies on its own), quotes no
accuracy figures, and does not say AI writes answers (answers are
evidence-only by default). The Content creators page is withdrawn: that
persona is hidden behind a module that is off by default (SHIP_DECISIONS
2026-10-23), so a store page for it would advertise something a new buyer
cannot see.

Why CPPs and not nine apps: App Review Guideline 4.3 (Spam) rejects
multiple bundle IDs of the same app and can terminate the developer
account on repeat violations. CPPs are Apple's sanctioned mechanism for
persona-targeted storefronts.
